```
Hello,

Thank you for the detailed feedback on Guideline 1.2. We have addressed all five
requirements in build 3. Below is where each one can be verified in the app.

1. AGREEMENT TO TERMS (EULA) BEFORE REGISTERING OR LOGGING IN
   The sign-in screen now shows a required consent checkbox directly above the
   submit button: "I have read and accept the Terms of Use and the Privacy
   Policy." Both are tappable links to our published documents.
   Until it is checked, ALL three entry paths are disabled: email sign-up /
   sign-in, Sign in with Apple, and Sign in with Google. The consent is stored
   with a version number and re-requested when the terms change.
   Terms of Use: https://yiitcan55.github.io/kurandakimesaj-legal/terms.html
   Our Terms include an explicit zero-tolerance clause for objectionable
   content and abusive users, with content removal and account termination as
   the stated consequence.

2. A METHOD FOR FILTERING OBJECTIONABLE CONTENT
   We implemented a pre-publication approval queue. Every user-submitted reel is
   created with status = 'pending' and is NOT visible to anyone except its own
   author until a moderator approves it. This is enforced at the database level
   (PostgreSQL row-level security plus a trigger), not only in the UI: a user
   cannot publish directly, cannot approve their own content, and cannot insert
   content in an already-approved state.
   After posting, the user sees a clear notice: "Your content is under review.
   It will appear in Reels once approved."

3. A MECHANISM FOR USERS TO FLAG OBJECTIONABLE CONTENT
   Every reel shows a labelled "Report" button directly in the action rail on
   the right side of the screen - it is visible without opening any menu. Every
   comment has a "..." menu that also contains "Report". Reports are stored
   server-side and surfaced in the in-app moderation screen, where a moderator
   can remove the reported post or delete the reported comment.

4. A MECHANISM FOR USERS TO BLOCK ABUSIVE USERS
   Next to "Report", each reel shows a labelled "Block" button (the comment
   "..." menu has the same action). Blocked users' content disappears from the
   feed immediately. Blocking is reversible: Settings > Blocked users lists them
   with an "Unblock" action.

5. ACTING ON REPORTS WITHIN 24 HOURS
   The app includes a moderator screen (Settings > Content moderation, visible
   only to admin accounts) with two tabs: "Pending" (approve / reject) and
   "Reports" (review the reported item and remove it). Because approval happens
   BEFORE publication, objectionable content does not reach other users while
   waiting for review.

blocking sign-in, (2) reporting a reel, and (3) blocking a user and the content
disappearing from the feed.

Additionally, in this build we added Sign in with Apple (Guideline 4.8), and we
would like to confirm regarding Guideline 5.2.3 that the app never downloads or
re-hosts third-party video. When a user pastes a social media link, we only read
the public preview image to identify the Qur'an verse in it; what the user can
then share is a card generated by our own app. Uploads are limited to the user's
own media, and both publishing paths require an explicit ownership declaration.

Thank you for your time.
```

3b. *(Güçlü ek)* Bir yorumda `⋯` → **Bildir**, sonra admin hesabıyla Ayarlar → İçerik moderasyonu → **Şikâyetler** → **Yorumu sil**.

## 5. Bu sürümde bilinçli olarak yapılmayanlar

Apple sorarsa dürüst cevap verebilmek için:

- **Gerçek video render** (ffmpeg/sunucu worker) — kapsam dışı. Stüdyo çıktısı `still` reel olarak yayınlanır; kullanıcıya "video üretiyoruz" vaadi verilmiyor (katalog metni buna göre düzeltildi).
- **Otomatik AI içerik moderasyonu** — manuel onay kuyruğu seçildi. Ölçek büyürse Edge Function'a taşınır.
- **iOS Share Extension** — macOS + Xcode gerektirir, `ios/SHARE_EXTENSION_SETUP.md` hazır. iOS kullanıcısı engellenmiyor: galeriden "Video Seç" ve "Bağlantı Yapıştır" yolları çalışıyor.
- **Android release keystore** — release build hâlâ debug keystore ile imzalanıyor. Play sürümü işi, App Store gönderimini bloklamaz. Gerçek keystore'a geçildiğinde Google Cloud'a yeni SHA-1 eklenmeli.
- **DNS rebinding'e karşı tam koruma** (link çözümlemede) — kalan risk kör SSRF; getirilen baytlar çağırana hiç dönmüyor ve `image/*` olmayan yanıt reddediliyor. Tam kapatma elle HTTP istemcisi yazmayı gerektirir, bir `og:image` getiricisi için orantısız. Kodda `ponytail:` yorumuyla işaretli.
